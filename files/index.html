------------------------------------------------------------
Baldur's Gate III
------------------------------------------------------------
[Mods are in recommended load order, and any marked "SE" require Norbyte's Script Extender.]

Valkrana's Spellbook
17 new signature necromancy spells, plus more undead options. Doesn't strictly require SE (except Greater Skeleport), but much better with.

Valkrana's Spellbook Limited Lists [Requires Spellbook]
Limits VS spells to Wiz, Bard, Sorc, Lock, EK, AT, a few modded classes, Death Cleric & Spores Druid if you have SE. Load after VS.

Valkrana's Impart Undeath Feat
The Valkrana's Impart Undeath spell as a feat.

Valkrana's Skeleton Crew Feat [Requires SE, Spellbook]
Get your very own random skeletal pal at the start of combat. Functionality tied to a feat.

Valkrana's Skeleton Crew [Requires SE, Spellbook]
Get your very own random skeletal pal at the start of combat.

Valkrana's Limited Edition Bone [Requires SE, Skeleton Crew]
Fantastic necromancer item! Highly-reviewed by those inured to undeath!

Valkrana's Skeletal Challenge [Requires SE, Skeleton Crew]
Difficulty mod that adds random undead enemies.

Valkrana's Undead Encounters [Requires SE, Skeleton Crew]
Adds undead map encounters across all acts.

Valkrana's Skeleton Crew XP Enabled [Requires SE, Skeleton Crew]
Skeleton Crew and Undead Encounters creatures yield XP.

Valkrana's Undead Champion [Requires SE, Spellbook]
Equippable undead warrior with its own inventory.

Necromancy Reanimated
Necromancy fixes, quality of life changes, and 5e adjustments.

Valkrana's Skeleton Emporium [Requires Skeleton Crew]
Over 50 new Animate Dead creatures.

Command Undead [Requires SE]
Missing capstone feature for the School of Necromancy wizard subclass.

Valkrana's Necrobroker [Requires SE, Command Undead]
Necromancy + divination wizard subclass.

Valkrana's Patron [Requires SE, Command Undead]
Valkrana's Necrobroker above as a warlock patron.

Wish [Requires SE]
Adds the ultimate spell, a staple in Valkrana's repertoire.

Arcanist Feat [Requires SE]
1st-level spell from any class, the Arcana skill, and +1 Intelligence.
